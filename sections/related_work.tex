\section{Related Work}
Researchers have conducted extensive studies about forest disturbance detection through satellite imaging which requires three specific methodologies: time series modeling and spectral analysis and unsupervised learning methods. The Sentinel-2 and Landsat sensors deliver continuous multi-spectral time series data which enables them to detect minor vegetation stress before it becomes visible to the human eye.
 
Recent systems have implemented cloud-native formats through their adoption of Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs) and PostgreSQL spatial databases combined with PostGIS. The technologies provide users with two key functions which allow them to filter spatial data across different time periods while accessing extensive geospatial information. Microsoft’s Planetary Computer and Radiant Earth’s STAC catalog demonstrate this systematized framework through their respective projects.

The project develops a spatiotemporal data cube through COG-based raster storage and PostGIS metadata indexing and the PySpark ETL pipeline. The system enables organizations to detect anomalies at scale through its combination of traditional and contemporary detection methods.
